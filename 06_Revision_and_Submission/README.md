# Editorial and Production Documentation

## Overview

This directory documents the editorial and production stages
of the research article published in the *Journal of Affective
Disorders Reports*.

It covers the transition from manuscript acceptance to final
publication, including editorial communication, production
processing, proofreading, and publication verification.

## Documentation

The associated research archive includes:

- **Editorial Correspondence:** Communications regarding
  manuscript acceptance and publication requirements.
- **Production Reports:** Documents related to journal
  production and manuscript processing.
- **Proofreading and Corrections:** Author-reviewed proofs,
  formatting corrections, and final verification.
- **Publication Records:** Final bibliographic information,
  DOI registration, and publication details.

## Document Availability

Editorial correspondence, internal production reports,
and publisher-generated proofs are maintained in a
separate private research archive.

These documents are not publicly redistributed without
appropriate authorization.
